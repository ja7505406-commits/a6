The Physics of Summit Survival

Alpine Layer Fox was founded by mountaineering veterans and textile physicists who understood that surviving extreme sub-zero alpine conditions requires a dynamic, multi-tier thermal ecosystem.

By engineering three complementary layers—hydrophobic merino wool against the skin, aerogel insulation in the core, and waterproof ePTFE hard shells on the exterior—our Manhattan atelier creates technical apparel that keeps climbers safe on the world's most formidable peaks.
