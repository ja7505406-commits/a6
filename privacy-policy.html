Privacy Policy & Patron Data Stewardship

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Alpine Layer Fox, Manhattan Atelier

1. Introduction & Mountaineering Confidentiality Mandate

At Alpine Layer Fox, operating our technical mountaineering atelier and high-altitude outfitting basecamp at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, we hold expedition confidentiality, biometric fitting profiles, and personal privacy to the highest standard of alpine outfitting stewardship. We recognize that commissioning bespoke 3-layer hard shells, scheduling private cold-chamber fittings, or ordering custom aerogel thermal suits involves sharing personal biometric measurements and confidential billing records. This Privacy Policy outlines the exact protocols we employ to collect, process, safeguard, and retain your data across our digital portals and Manhattan showroom.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you engage with our technical atelier, we collect essential client information including your full name, email address, telephone number, billing records, custom garment sizing parameters, and expedition destination profiles recorded during private salon viewings. Additionally, when you utilize our interactive alpine layering configurator or browse our mountaineering research treatises, our web servers record technical telemetry such as IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and referral paths via Google Tag manager. These technical metrics enable our digital textile engineers to optimize browsing performance and protect transaction security across international mountaineering markets.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate artisan manufacturing and commercial operations, including fulfilling custom outerwear commissions, coordinating salon fittings, organizing personalized garment monogramming, generating formal invoices, and delivering insured armored delivery tracking updates. Furthermore, with your explicit consent, we may send private invitations to closed-door alpine gear reveals and new high-altitude membrane prototype previews. We maintain an absolute policy never to sell, rent, monetize, or publicly distribute mountaineer identities or expedition dossiers to any third-party marketing brokers.

4. Data Storage & Cryptographic Protection Standards

All digital expedition sizing archives, custom pattern files, and billing information entrusted to Alpine Layer Fox are stored on private cloud servers shielded by 256-bit AES encryption protocols and strict administrative access controls. Digital payment transactions are handled directly through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant processors, ensuring our atelier never retains raw credit card numbers on local hardware. We perform continuous vulnerability assessments and cybersecurity audits to guarantee that your private information remains completely protected from unauthorized external access.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Access Controls

Regardless of your country of residence, Alpine Layer Fox extends comprehensive data privacy rights aligned with global standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You retain the absolute legal right to inspect the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to profile records, restrict processing activities, or demand the permanent deletion of your expedition records from our systems. To exercise these statutory privacy rights, simply submit a formal request to our concierge desk at any time.

6. Data Retention Protocols & Archival Integrity

We retain client bespoke outerwear pattern files and expedition sizing measurements for as long as your patron profile remains active, enabling seamless repeat orders, seasonal layering updates, and lifetime technical repair services without requiring repetitive fitting sessions. If you choose to conclude your patronage or request data expungement, all electronic records and custom garment cutting programs are securely purged and permanently deleted within thirty business days, subject only to mandatory statutory tax and accounting retention requirements mandated by United States commercial law.

7. Atelier Contact for Privacy Governance

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to discuss our client confidentiality practices with our leadership team, please contact our administrative desk. Written correspondence should be addressed directly to Alpine Layer Fox, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically to our concierge staff by calling +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours. Our compliance committee will examine your communication with professional care and respond within standard commercial timelines.
